## Detailed Findings

### Critical (Must Fix)

#### 1. [R-25 / Contrast] Primary brand color fails WCAG AA contrast ratio against white text
- **File & Lines:** `index.html` (line 34), `components/ProfileCard.tsx` (line 55), `components/SetPinModal.tsx` (line 60), `components/ProgressBar.tsx` (line 11), `pages/HomePage.tsx` (lines 120, 170)
- **Current Code:** Configured with indigo-500 (`#6366f1`), producing a 4.47:1 contrast ratio against `#ffffff` (fails WCAG AA threshold of 4.5:1).
- **Required Fix:** Upgrade primary interactive and badge backgrounds to indigo-600 (`#4f46e5`, contrast 6.29:1: passes WCAG AA).

#### 2. [R-32 / Accessibility] Removal of focus outlines via `focus:outline-none` without keyboard focus rings
- **File & Lines:**
  - `components/LockScreen.tsx` (lines 74, 103)
  - `components/SetPinModal.tsx` (lines 70, 103)
  - `components/ProfileCard.tsx` (lines 69, 82, 94, 106, 120)
  - `components/MeasurementRow.tsx` (line 79)
  - `components/FAB.tsx` (line 15)
  - `components/AppSettings.tsx` (line 56)
- **Current Code:** Strips standard browser outlines (`focus:outline-none`, `focus:ring-0`), leaving keyboard/screen-reader users without visual cues.
- **Required Fix:** Replace with explicit focus rings: `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-indigo-600 focus-visible:ring-offset-2`.

#### 3. [R-32 / Accessibility] Modals lack Escape key listener, backdrop focus trapping, and ARIA dialog semantics
- **File & Lines:**
  - `components/SetPinModal.tsx` (line 77)
  - `components/AddPhotoModal.tsx` (line 41)
  - `components/ViewPhotoModal.tsx` (line 28)
  - `components/CustomMeasurementModal.tsx` (line 40)
- **Current Code:** Modal dialogs cannot be dismissed with the <kbd>Escape</kbd> key, lack `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, and `aria-labelledby`.
- **Required Fix:** Implement a keydown listener for `Escape`, add semantic ARIA attributes, and ensure tap-to-dismiss on backdrop.

#### 4. [C-2 / Functional Completeness & R-26] Abandoned zero-byte empty files in codebase
- **File & Lines:**
  - `components/AddMeasurementModal.tsx` (0 bytes)
  - `components/MetricInsightModal.tsx` (0 bytes)
  - `pages/RecordEntryPage.tsx` (0 bytes)
- **Current Code:** Leftover empty files with no implementation and no imports.
- **Required Fix:** Safely remove all three empty stub files to clean up project architecture.

#### 5. [R-03 / Mobile Layout] Interactive buttons and form controls fail minimum 44px tap target size
- **File & Lines:**
  - `components/SecuritySettings.tsx` (lines 48, 52: `px-3 py-1 text-sm` ~28px height; lines 72, 77: text buttons with no padding ~20px)
  - `components/Calendar.tsx` (line 114: day buttons `w-10 h-10` = 40px)
  - `components/ThemeToggle.tsx` (line 11: `w-14 h-8` = 32px height)
  - `components/AppSettings.tsx` (lines 43, 56: select and switch with small hit areas)
  - `components/SetPinModal.tsx` & `components/AddPhotoModal.tsx` (close buttons `h-6 w-6` = 24px)
- **Current Code:** Controls are significantly smaller than the 44px mobile touch standard, leading to mis-clicks.
- **Required Fix:** Expand padding and minimum hit areas to at least 44px × 44px (`min-h-[44px] min-w-[44px] flex items-center justify-center`).

### Major (Should Fix)

- **File & Lines:** `components/AIInsights.tsx` (line 62)
- **Current Code:** `bg-gradient-to-r from-indigo-500 to-purple-600` (generic AI gradient trope).
- **Required Fix:** Replace with high-contrast, purposeful solid button styling (`bg-indigo-600 hover:bg-indigo-700 text-white font-medium`).

#### 7. [R-04 / Icons] Sparkles icon AI cliché
- **File & Lines:** `components/AIInsights.tsx` (line 63), `components/Icons.tsx` (line 88)
- **Current Code:** 4-point magic sparkle icon used for AI generation trigger.
- **Required Fix:** Replace with a purposeful analytical icon (e.g. `TrendingUp` or `ChartIcon`) or clean typographic action.

#### 8. [R-12 / Shadow & R-14 / Layout] Heavy shadow stacking and card repetition
- **File & Lines:** `pages/HomePage.tsx` (lines 48, 64: `shadow-lg rounded-xl`), `components/GoalTracker.tsx`
- **Current Code:** Monotonous stacking of `shadow-lg` and `shadow-xl` across consecutive home screen cards.
- **Required Fix:** Modernize elevation with refined borders (`border border-gray-100 dark:border-gray-800`), crisp subtle shadows (`shadow-sm`), and intentional padding rhythm.

#### 10. [R-22 / Copywriting] Sycophantic and formulaic AI cheerleading prompts

#### 12. [Usability / File Upload] Image upload lacks drag-and-drop support

### Minor (Polish)

#### 15. [R-26 / Usability] Native `window.confirm` browser dialogs
- **File & Lines:** `components/ViewPhotoModal.tsx` (line 17), `components/GoalTracker.tsx` (line 30)
- **Current Code:** Relies on browser-native `window.confirm()` which can fail in iframe contexts or disrupt UI flow.
